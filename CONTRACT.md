# Product contract — Handset

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Handset is the complete business phone system and SMB cloud contact center a US business buys instead of RingCentral, Dialpad, Aircall or Zoom Phone, and a credible destination for Quo, CloudTalk, JustCall, Newo, Phonely and Beside Phone buyers. The 5–100 seat contact-center buyer may also switch from Talkdesk or Five9 for the voice job. There is no released contract or product implementation: every clause is a target obligation and **missing**, including acceptance evidence that builders must produce. A provider-schema fake proves protocol handling, never real carrier activation, emergency delivery, app-store distribution, a real account migration or a human receiving a call.

The job is to own business numbers, make the right person or approved receptionist answer, preserve what was said under consent, and finish the customer’s requested work. US-first means USD, a 5–200 person US first buyer, US/Canada calling and numbers, US timezones and law-aware policies. Difficulty in carrier relationships, filings, regulated payment capture, native apps, model evaluation or independent routes is engineering work required by named clauses, never a reason to narrow the destination. Partner-controlled outcomes and unsupported source fields remain visible until resolved; an honest state is not permission to omit the promised capability.

## Acceptance conventions

Each clause names buyer behavior and a public-door journey. Use actual product routes, UI/API/CLI and real receiving-system readback; fake only the network/paid-provider edge from that provider’s current official schema. Authorized live carrier, native OS, recipient and authentic-source migration evidence is required wherever a fake cannot establish the result. Use dedicated controlled numbers and synthetic clinic/card content in tests, never unsolicited emergency calls or unconsented customer outreach. The named timings and scale are targets to measure, not current performance assertions.

At **1440 and 390 pixels**, use realistic populated US/English/Spanish data and inspect every relevant screen and successful, empty, pending, rejected, permission-denied, stale and failed end state. Native iOS/Android checks require real devices in addition to responsive browser journeys. Evidence includes request/receipt identifiers, source counts, denied actions and actual outcome, with secrets/customer-sensitive content removed. No screen, workflow, code or marketing promise is delivered by this contract-writing task.

All outbound work rechecks current consent, recipient-local hours, applicable jurisdiction, active grant, exact approval and budget at effect time. “Queued,” “provider accepted,” “carrier delivered,” “answered,” “human acknowledged” and “customer outcome confirmed” are separate facts. A lost response after a possibly committed side effect is uncertain until readback or explicit resolution. No unqualified carrier/model response can make a clause done.
