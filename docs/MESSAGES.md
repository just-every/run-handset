# Shared business messages

Open `/app/messages` as a current native owner. Claim a conversation, review its inbound messages and attachment metadata, and manage saved snippets and explicit customer-care permissions under Message settings. `/app/messages/settings` is the configuration door. The current owner-only boundary is an adopted conservative default pending ct; it does not replace the broader scoped administration target.

A claim compares the current thread revision and can win once. Recording HappyToHelp custody clears the native claimant, suppresses unsent drafts and queued messages, and preserves history. This records the native handoff boundary; it does not contact HappyToHelp or prove that its remote support system accepted a handoff. Returning custody produces an unclaimed thread requiring a fresh claim and fresh draft approval.

A saved snippet creates an immutable message draft. The review shows its exact body, attachment references and due time. A separate approval binds that message to the current owner version, thread revision, explicit recipient permission and reviewed source policy. Scheduling is not a send. Execution independently checks current toll-free verification, recipient-local hours, source authority, current owner and permission, custody and company-wide STOP. A changed approval or custody cannot authorize the old draft.

The current one-to-one route permits SMS and bounded JPEG, PNG and GIF MMS references already received for that exact native source. Before submission, provider media metadata must match the approved identity and MIME type. The authenticated media body is then read within the approved byte bound and must measure exactly the approved byte count; caller declarations and length headers cannot substitute for that measurement. Missing, mismatched, oversized or redirected media is refused before creating a provider message. Bytes are discarded after verification and are never stored or previewed. The native route caps the complete message at ten segments, ten attachments and five million total attachment bytes. Incoming content is metadata only; it is not downloaded or played. New upload and attachment-preview doors remain unbuilt. A Twilio message create occurs once after an atomic claim; a lost response is uncertain and is never automatically submitted again. Independent readback checks account, exact sender, recipient, body, direction and attachment count. Provider acceptance and carrier delivery remain separate; neither implies a read receipt. Older provider states cannot regress a delivered result.

Signed inbound STOP, its documented keyword equivalents and the official `OptOutType=STOP` field persist a company-wide stop. The stop transaction suppresses unsent inbox work across every company number. Call-bound care and survey texts use the same suppression query. Existing scoped connected application preparation, replay and execution also refuse a stopped party observed in their selected native case. A current scoped credential can query `/api/handset/v1/cases/{id}/message-suppression` for an exact selected case under its existing `message.send` scope. The check returns no recipient data and grants no sending authority. Direct native recipient and thread suppression checks require an owner session. This is a native gate available to connected systems; independent Mango receipt and actual third-party enforcement remain unproved.

No call, inbound message, bare START keyword or newly recorded permission clears STOP. Re-opt-in requires its own verifiable evidence and reviewed policy before such a door can ship. Native sends never bypass STOP for confirmation or help. Required replies are provider-managed only in this slice; the single permitted confirmation and separate approved legal policy still need qualification.

## Group metadata boundary

Twilio Programmable Messaging is one-to-one. Group fields on its ordinary ingress are refused rather than flattened into individual recipients. The separate `/providers/twilio/messages/groups` door accepts signed Conversations `onMessageAdded` notifications only for an owner-authorized exact service, conversation and business source. It independently reads the conversation, complete participant list and message from the provider. It checks account and service identity, active conversation, one business projected address, the exact participant author, and bounded media metadata. Pagination, ambiguous membership, foreign authors, changed replay content and withdrawn authority are refused. No media bytes are fetched.

This receive-only route supports at most ten total participants on US and Canada long codes. Toll-free numbers cannot be authorized for it. An owner can withdraw the exact group source in Message settings; subsequent ingress and thread reads are refused. A signed verified group STOP also records the same company-wide stop. Group sending remains refused: the held account has no approved long-code messaging registration, and a real group receiver is still required. This is not a claim of group carrier qualification.

The group representation follows the official [Group Texting guide](https://www.twilio.com/docs/conversations-classic/group-texting), [participant resource](https://www.twilio.com/docs/conversations-classic/api/conversation-participant-resource), [message resource](https://www.twilio.com/docs/conversations-classic/api/conversation-message-resource) and [webhook reference](https://www.twilio.com/docs/conversations-classic/conversations-webhooks). It uses the stricter ten-participant bound in the guide and limits page. Ordinary media and opt-out fields follow the [incoming webhook reference](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/guides/webhook-request) and [Messaging TwiML](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/twiml).
